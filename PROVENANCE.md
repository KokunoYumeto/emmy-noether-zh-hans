# Decision provenance

The current complete public decision-provenance bundle is released with version 1.0.2 at [10.5281/zenodo.22172487](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22172487) as `05_NOETHER_zh-Hans_PROVENANCE.zip`.

It contains the producer and independent-checker decision logs through the frozen r10b release, exact freeze and acceptance receipts, manifests, and publication/backend verification evidence. An internal manifest binds every bundled copy to its path, byte count, SHA-256 identity, and local source preimage.

For privacy, textual copies replace the local account holder's first name with `[USER]`. The unredacted source-preimage hashes are retained, but credentials, caches, render dumps, and other disposable machine-local material are excluded. This privacy transform does not alter the Chinese corpus, mathematics, German authority, or release PDFs.
