## English identification

This repository is the standalone PRC-oriented Simplified-Chinese (`zh-Hans-CN`) edition of the complete maintained Emmy Noether corpus. Its fixed scope comprises 43 numbered papers, two separately identified post-numbered works/scopes, and terminal bibliography/editorial apparatus, bound to German authority v047/ED0011. The public reader adds one DOI cover to the unchanged 424-page machine-validated r10b body.

“Complete” describes declared corpus coverage. It does not claim infallibility, a critical edition, or external/human certification. Traditional Chinese, Singapore localization, and Taiwan/Hong Kong/Macao localizations are outside this release.
